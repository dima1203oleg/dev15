About SirenUA

We are a dedicated Ukrainian software team building mission-critical AI applications for civilian protection.

Our Mission

Every Second Saves Lives

SirenUA was founded in direct response to the urgent realities of wartime Ukraine. Millions of civilians need immediate, accurate, and actionable warnings about incoming threats in their local sectors. We harness artificial intelligence to reduce alert delay from minutes to seconds.

Our guiding principle: core emergency safety functions — sirens, threat notifications, and shelter routing — will always remain free for everyone.
