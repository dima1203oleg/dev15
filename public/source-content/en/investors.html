The Problem

Seconds Matter When Saving Lives

Ukraine faces regular combined strikes consisting of ballistic missiles, cruise missiles, loitering munitions, and guided aerial bombs. Flight times for hypersonic and ballistic weapons can be as short as a few minutes, making instant warnings critical.

  • 💔 High civilian risk across urban and rural population centers
  • ⏱️ Urgent demand for instant identification of weapon classes and flight trajectories
  • 🏚️ Lack of turn-by-turn routing to certified underground safe spaces
  • 📊 Need for predictive modeling of recurring attack corridors
The Solution

Sub-3-Second AI Threat Processing

SirenUA processes live battlefield intelligence through a custom neural pipeline. The system classifies incoming threats, kinematics, flight vectors, and target regions, dispatching critical alerts in under 3 seconds.

  • 🧠 Self-calibrating AI heuristics refined after every attack session
  • ⚡ Ingestion-to-notification latency under 3 seconds
  • 🛡️ Pre-seeded civil defense registry with verified safety tier routing
  • 📊 Probabilistic route chain modeling across all 27 regions
