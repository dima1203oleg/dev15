2. Description of Service

SirenUA provides real-time digital intelligence and early warning signals regarding aerial hazards over Ukraine, including:

  • Critical Alert notifications for official sirens and AI-detected threats
  • Interactive geospatial mapping of live threat vectors
  • Civil defense shelter registry and walking turn-by-turn routing
  • Extended ballistic analytics (available with a Premium subscription)

3. Important Safety Warning & Disclaimer

⚠️ SirenUA is a supplementary AI-driven warning tool and does not replace official state civil defense alert systems. Always follow the directives of the State Emergency Service of Ukraine (DSNS) and the Air Force Command of the Armed Forces of Ukraine.

4. Access & Subscriptions
