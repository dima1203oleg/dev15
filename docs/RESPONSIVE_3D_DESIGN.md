# Responsive 3D Design

The visual target is the supplied SIREN UA direction: a layered transparent map stack with trajectory, risk, personal region and shelter layers.

- Desktop: monitor-centered intelligence view with side situation and partner panels.
- Laptop: central product view; secondary panels collapse below.
- Tablet: touch-first two-column/stacked situation view; controls remain at least 44px.
- Mobile: short safety promise, primary download CTA, reduced layer count, simplified shadows and reduced animation.

CSS 3D is preferred for the current stack. WebGL is not required until it delivers measurable product value. `prefers-reduced-motion` should disable parallax, rotation and pulsing effects.
