# UX Architecture

The product follows the information order: **what is happening → where → confidence/freshness → what to do next**.

- Public: product promise, 3D Intelligence Stack, current-data state, product features, download and partner CTA.
- Map: region state, event category, direction, ETA range, confidence, timestamp and shelters.
- Partner: L1/L2 network, rank progress, earnings buckets and payout status.
- Admin: integration health, cap validation, audit, fraud and reconciliation.

Safety copy must never make a forecast sound guaranteed. Official alerts remain primary.
